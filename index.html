Asymptotic Hub

Asymptotic Hub is a private internal record-keeping tool for Asymptotic Systems Private Limited, an Indian private limited company.

It reads the Gmail accounts of the company and its directors, each with that account owner's permission and read-only access, so that the directors can keep company documents, deadlines and records organized in one place. It is used only by the company's directors. It is not a public product and has no public users.
